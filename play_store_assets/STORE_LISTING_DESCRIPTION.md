## 📝 Full Description (Markdown Format for testmybuild.app)

GupShupGo is a fast, secure, feature-rich messaging and calling app designed for real-time communication, total privacy, and fun interactive experiences for GenZ users who value privacy.

### 🌐 OFFLINE NEARBY MESH CHAT (NO INTERNET NEEDED)
• Send messages & media nearby even when internet or cellular data is unavailable
• Direct peer-to-peer Bluetooth & Wi-Fi Direct mesh networking up to 100 meters
• Ideal for flights, concerts, underground travel, and remote outdoor trips
• Offline messages automatically sync back online once your connection returns

### 🔒 END-TO-END ENCRYPTION (E2EE)
• Signal Protocol military-grade end-to-end cryptographic privacy
• Private 1-on-1 chats and calls remain completely encrypted
• Instant Safety Numbers cryptographic identity verification
• Zero server-side message storage for encrypted conversations

### 🤖 GupAI — YOUR PERSONAL IN-APP AI ASSISTANT
• Need help drafting a reply, summarizing notes, or brainstorming? Ask GupAI!
• Fast, context-aware answers built directly into your messenger
• Instant translation across languages right inside your chat workflow
• Daily free quota with easy top-ups

### 🦅 ANONYMOUS CHATTING & RANDOM MATCHING
• Connect instantly with random users around the world
• Random fun pseudonym assignment (e.g. Clever Eagle 🦅)
• Custom anonymous avatars with 100% identity protection
• Safe, fun, and effortless social discovery

### 💬 INSTANT & FAST MESSAGING
• Send text messages, photos, videos, audio notes, and media files
• Voice messages with high-quality audio & playback controls
• Real-time typing indicators and online/offline status
• Delete messages anytime for everyone or locally

### 📞 HD VOICE & VIDEO CALLS (PiP & SCREEN SHARING)
• Crystal-clear, low-latency voice and video calls
• In-call Screen Sharing: share slides, photos, or your screen in real time
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP) support: keep talking while multitasking across other apps
• In-call controls: toggle camera, mute mic, flip camera, speaker mode, and call hold
• Low-bandwidth mode optimized for 2G/3G networks

### 🛡️ PRIVATE VAULT & BIOMETRIC CHAT LOCK
